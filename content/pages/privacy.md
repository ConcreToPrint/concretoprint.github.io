This website is run by the ConcreTOPrint student team of Politecnico di Torino. Contact: {{ email_link() }}. It is not an official Politecnico di Torino website.

**No cookies, no tracking.** This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ads.

**Server logs.** Like every website, our hosting provider automatically records technical data such as IP addresses, only to deliver the site and keep it secure. These logs are deleted automatically.

**Emails and applications.** If you email us or apply through our Google Form, we use your details only to reply or to evaluate your application, and we delete them when no longer needed. The form is hosted by Google, whose [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies.

**Your rights.** You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at the address above, and you can complain to the Italian data protection authority ([Garante privacy](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).
